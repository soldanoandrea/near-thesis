## (d) Staking yield and real yield

**Definitions.** Gross staker yield = issuance x (1 - treasury share) / staking ratio; net = gross x (1 - commission); float dilution = issuance - burn - buybacks; real staker yield = (1 + net) / (1 + float dilution) - 1 (change in a staker's share of the float); non-staker real yield = 1 / (1 + float dilution) - 1. Commission is not measured here: [0.0, 0.05, 0.1] are shown.
